Subject: Introducing the SAP Courses App — Centralized Training Management on SAP Fiori

Dear Leadership Team,

I am pleased to present the SAP Courses application — a purpose-built training management solution developed on our SAP S/4HANA platform and deployed through SAP Fiori. This application streamlines how BridgeBio tracks, assigns, and monitors SAP learning across the organization.

THE CHALLENGE
As our SAP footprint grows, ensuring that every team member completes the right training at the right time has become increasingly complex. Spreadsheet-based tracking, manual follow-ups, and limited visibility into completion status create bottlenecks for both managers and end users.

THE SOLUTION
The SAP Courses App provides a single, integrated platform accessible directly from the SAP Fiori Launchpad — a standard Fiori tile, no separate login or external tools required.

FOR MANAGERS & ADMINISTRATORS
  • Team Analytics Dashboard — Real-time KPIs: Total, Pending, In Progress, Overdue, and Completed assignments across the team
  • Training Assignment — Assign courses to specific team members with due dates and progress tracking
  • Smart Filtering — Dependent dropdown filters (Role → Topic → Module) that dynamically narrow options
  • De-assignment & Reassignment — Flexible management as roles evolve
  • Export Reports — One-click Excel download of training data for leadership reporting
  • Email Notifications — Send assignment and reminder notifications directly from the app

FOR END USERS
  • My Assignments — Personalized view of all assigned courses with status tracking
  • Start Training — One-click launch; status automatically updates to "In Progress"
  • Mark Completed — Self-service completion with automatic timestamp and audit trail
  • Card & Table Views — Toggle between a visual card layout and a detailed data table with built-in scrollbar

END-TO-END WORKFLOW DEMONSTRATED
The attached video walkthrough covers the complete lifecycle:
  1. Launch the app from the SAP Fiori Launchpad tile
  2. Review Team Analytics KPIs and open the Analytics Dashboard
  3. Use Smart Filters to narrow courses by Role, Topic, and Module
  4. Toggle between Card View and Table View
  5. Navigate to My Assignments and assign a training to a team member
  6. Start Training (Assigned → In Progress) and Mark Completed (In Progress → Completed)
  7. Verify completion status end-to-end using the status filter

TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
  • Built on SAPUI5 / SAP Fiori with OData V2 services via SAP Gateway
  • Role-based access control (Admin / Manager / End User)
  • Responsive design — optimized for desktops, laptops, and tablets
  • Deployed on SAP Fiori Launchpad as a standard Fiori tile
